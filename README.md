# Project title

E-commerce Customer Intelligence & Retention Analysis

# Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior based on RFM, retention patterns, and revenue trends using transactional e-commerce data. The goal is to generate actionable insights that support business growth and customer retention strategies.

# Business Problem

The company wants to understand:
- Which customer segment generate the most revenue and Which segment are below expectation ?
- How customer retention changes over time ?
- Which products drive sales growth ?
- Opportunities to improve customer loyalty ?

# Key Insights

- Around 26% of Customers are comes under Lost / Hibernating segment.
- Most revenue driven by Champion Segment (46%) across different customer segments.
- January 22.18% and February 20.24% showed highest retention rate across cohort months.
- The City Kayseri and Eskiseher showed 28% of lost/hibernating customers and Bursa Contributing 20.97% of Champion segment.
- the product category Sports , Books and Beauty drives the sales.


# Recommendations
- A Re-activation campaign is needed in kayseri and Eskiseher For Re-gaining Hibernating customers.
- Prioritize and retain High value customers including Champion, Loyal Segments through quick access, discounts and fulfilment.
- Promote high-performing product categories including sports, Books and beauty.
- Reduce time-to-first-purchase through onboarding incentives.
